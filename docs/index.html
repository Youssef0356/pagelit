Tools that stay quiet.

Pagelit is built to be used, not to get in the way. Everything you need to read and mark up a document — nothing you don't.

Smooth PDF reading

Fast, crisp viewer with pinch-zoom, a page scrubber and jump-to-page. Pick up where you left off — reading progress is saved per document.

Highlight & mark up

Highlight, underline, strikethrough and squiggly marks in 7 colours. Tap any mark to recolour or delete it.

Comment anywhere

Drop a sticky-note comment on any spot in the page. A comments bottom sheet lists them all — tap one to jump back.

Find text

In-document search with a match counter and next / previous navigation. Results highlight on the page.

Full-screen reading

One tap hides the app bar, the toolbar and the system bars so the page gets the whole screen. Tap again to bring the tools back.

Share & export

Share the annotated PDF through any app, or save a separate annotated copy to a location you choose.

Sepia & dim

A warm sepia tint or a dim overlay for night reading, and a keep-screen-on switch — all from Settings.

Save pages to gallery

Export your current view as an image straight into your photo gallery. Useful for notes, slides and sharing a single page.

Light, dark or system

Pick the theme once in Settings. Reading position and recents stay on your device.

Opens from anywhere

Tap a PDF in an email, file manager or browser and choose Pagelit. Your document opens instantly.

Small banner. No pop-ups.

Pagelit is funded by a single, non-disruptive banner ad. No interstitials, no paywalls, no “unlock editing” screens — just a reader that gets out of your way.
