1. Who we are

Pagelit is a free PDF reader for Android, published by The356Company (nejiyoussef081@gmail.com). Pagelit is a local, offline-first app: the PDFs you open are read and annotated on your device, and they are never uploaded to us or to anyone else by Pagelit.

2. Information collected

We collect no information directly ourselves. The following is collected by the third-party services built into the app:

2.1 Files and documents you open

When you open a PDF, Pagelit reads it using the Syncfusion PDF toolkit, which runs locally on your device. Pagelit does not read or upload the contents of your file to any server. Highlights, underlines, strike-throughs, squiggly marks and sticky-note comments you add live in memory while the document is open, and can be written into a separate annotated copy of the PDF that you choose to save — that copy stays wherever you put it.

A short list of recently opened files and your reading progress (current page and total pages) are stored on your device through Android's shared preferences, so you can reopen documents and resume where you left off. Neither is uploaded anywhere. You can remove all of it at once from Settings → Clear recents and progress, or by clearing the app's data in Android settings.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pagelit is free and supported by a single small banner ad, shown by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google may collect and process information about your device and how you interact with ads so the ad can be displayed and measured. This may include:

  • Device information — device type and model, operating system and version, language, screen size.
  • Advertising identifier — your resettable Android Advertising ID, if you have not disabled advertising personalization.
  • Network and location signals — IP address, approximate geographic location derived from it, mobile network operator.
  • Ad interactions — ad impressions, clicks and basic performance measurements.

Google uses this information as an independent data controller. For the full list of data it processes and for how it uses it, see Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob section of Google's support documentation (support.google.com/admob). Pagelit does not receive any of this data; it is exchanged directly between your device and Google.

Personalized advertising. Google may personalize the banner using your advertising ID, where allowed in your region. You are in control:

  • Android settings → Google → Ads → turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization; or
  • visit adssettings.google.com and myadcenter.google.com.

You can also reset your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings. If you reset or delete your advertising ID, ad requests may still be served, but without being tied to a persistent identifier.

2.3 Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos / media — only when you save a page to your gallery. Pagelit writes that one image and nothing else.
  • Internet — used solely to load the banner ad. The reader itself works fully offline.
  • Keep screen awake — an optional setting that stops the screen sleeping while a document is open. It collects nothing.

Pagelit requests no access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or files other than the one you explicitly pick.

2.4 Information we do not collect

Pagelit has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal information. There is no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK in Pagelit beyond Google AdMob.

2.5 Gallery exports and sharing

When you save a page to your gallery or share an annotated PDF, Pagelit uses Android's system services. The destination app or folder you choose receives the file — its handling of that file is governed by that app's own privacy practices, not ours.

3. How we use information

The only use of your data is:

  • to operate the app (open, render, annotate and share PDFs);
  • to serve and measure the small banner advertisement;
  • to respond to your support email if you contact us.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal information.

The only third party that processes data on our behalf is Google AdMob, described above. Pagelit itself has no server and no database; nothing you open or annotate is transmitted by us.

5. Children

Pagelit is not directed at children under 13 and is not a “mixed-audience” or kids app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used Pagelit and provided information to us, please contact us and we will delete that information.

6. Data retention & security

Recents and reading progress live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app, clear its data, or use Settings → Clear recents and progress. Ad-related data is retained according to Google's own policies. We apply standard care measures commensurate with a local-only app.

Pagelit requires an internet connection for the banner ad to load; the reader itself works fully offline.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you;
  • restrict, object to or withdraw consent for certain processing;
  • request portability of your data;
  • complain to your local data-protection authority.

Because Pagelit does not hold a data record of yours (we have no server), most of these rights are effectively satisfied by the data residing on your device (local recents, reading progress) which you control directly. For rights concerning ad data, exercise them via Google's tools linked in section 2.2. Contact us at nejiyoussef081@gmail.com for anything else.

Residents of California (CCPA / CPRA). We do not sell or share personal information. You may opt out of any targeted-ad processing via the Google tools in section 2.2 and you will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Residents of the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR). Google processes advertising data based on its own legal grounds, described in Google's Privacy Policy. Our legal basis for the app itself is necessity for providing the service you request.

8. International transfers

Pagelit runs on your device. Ad-related requests are sent to Google, who may process them in countries where their infrastructure is located, under the safeguards they publish (including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published at this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app where possible. Please review it periodically.
